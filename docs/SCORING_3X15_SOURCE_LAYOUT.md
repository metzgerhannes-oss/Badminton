# 3x15 and source match cards

Stand 11.10.2026. BWF and DBV change individual tournaments to two winning games of 15 points starting January 2027. At 14-all the winner needs a lead of two, maximum 21. Historical youth formats of 11/15 remain supported.

Six of Philipp's public Badhub match cards have complete 15-point results outside the ordinary result column. Reading only that column incorrectly classified these as unplayed; the update checks the complete card and still verifies both players, point results, and source winner. It does not turn a BYE/Rast or walkover into a win.
